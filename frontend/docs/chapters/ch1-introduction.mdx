# Introduction to Physical AI

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift in artificial intelligence, moving beyond purely digital systems to AI that interacts with and manipulates the physical world. This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts that form the foundation of modern robotics and embodied intelligence.

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that:

- **Perceive** the physical environment through sensors
- **Reason** about spatial relationships and physical interactions
- **Act** upon the world through actuators and motors
- **Learn** from physical experiences and feedback

Unlike traditional AI that operates purely in the digital realm, Physical AI must contend with the messiness, uncertainty, and real-time demands of the physical world.

## The Embodiment Hypothesis

The embodiment hypothesis suggests that true intelligence requires a physical body. Key aspects include:

1. **Sensorimotor Integration**: Intelligence emerges from the tight coupling of sensing and acting
2. **Environmental Interaction**: Learning happens through direct physical manipulation
3. **Morphological Computation**: The body itself can perform computation

> "Intelligence cannot be separated from the body that hosts it." — Rolf Pfeifer

## Historical Context

The field of Physical AI has evolved through several key phases:

| Era | Focus | Key Development |
|-----|-------|-----------------|
| 1950s-1970s | Symbolic AI | Logic-based reasoning |
| 1980s-1990s | Behavior-based | Reactive architectures |
| 2000s-2010s | Statistical | Machine learning integration |
| 2020s+ | Foundation Models | Large-scale learning for robotics |

## Core Components of Physical AI Systems

### Sensing

Physical AI systems rely on various sensors:

- **Vision**: Cameras, depth sensors, LiDAR
- **Touch**: Force sensors, tactile arrays
- **Proprioception**: Joint encoders, IMUs
- **Proximity**: Ultrasonic, infrared sensors

### Computation

Modern Physical AI leverages:

```
Perception → Planning → Control → Action
     ↑                              |
     └──────── Feedback ────────────┘
```

### Actuation

Converting decisions into physical motion through:

- Electric motors (servo, stepper, brushless)
- Hydraulic systems
- Pneumatic actuators
- Soft robotics materials

## Why Physical AI Matters

Physical AI enables robots to:

1. **Assist humans** in daily tasks and dangerous environments
2. **Automate manufacturing** with flexibility and precision
3. **Explore** environments inaccessible to humans
4. **Provide care** for aging populations
