# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the **AI-Native Interactive Textbook** on Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. This comprehensive resource will guide you through the fundamentals of embodied artificial intelligence and the engineering of human-like robots.

## What You'll Learn

This textbook covers six essential topics:

| Chapter | Topic | Key Concepts |
|---------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | [Introduction to Physical AI](/chapters/ch1-introduction) | Embodiment, perception-action loop |
| 2 | [Humanoid Robotics Fundamentals](/chapters/ch2-humanoid-fundamentals) | Mechanics, actuation, balance |
| 3 | [Sensors and Perception](/chapters/ch3-sensors-perception) | Vision, touch, sensor fusion |
| 4 | [Motion Planning and Control](/chapters/ch4-motion-planning) | Path planning, feedback control |
| 5 | [AI Integration](/chapters/ch5-ai-integration) | Deep learning, foundation models |
| 6 | [Real-World Applications](/chapters/ch6-applications) | Industry, healthcare, home |

## Features

This isn't just a textbook - it's an **AI-native learning experience**:

- **Interactive Q&A**: Ask questions and get answers grounded in the textbook content
- **Personalized Content**: Adjust the complexity based on your background level
- **Urdu Translation**: Read in English or Urdu
- **Chapter Quizzes**: Test your understanding with interactive quizzes

## Getting Started

1. **Browse Chapters**: Use the sidebar to navigate to any chapter
2. **Ask the AI**: Use the chatbot to ask questions about the content
3. **Take Quizzes**: Test your knowledge after each chapter
4. **Customize**: Set your background level for personalized explanations

## About This Project

This textbook is part of an AI-powered education platform designed to make learning about robotics accessible to everyone. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced engineer, you'll find value in these materials.
